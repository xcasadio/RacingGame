# ADR-0001: RacingGameCasaEngine screens are CasaEngine screen assets bound to view models

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-10-05
- **Source**: this chantier: `ai-agent/tasks/rgce-xaml-screens-tasks.md`, decisions D1-D17 (author answers, 2026-10-05); CasaEngine `docs/decisions/0035-xaml-authored-ui-screens.md` and `docs/decisions/0038-game-screens-are-assets-bound-to-view-models.md`

## Context

- The 9 MGUI screens of RacingGameCasaEngine (Splash, MainMenu, CarSelection, TrackSelection, Options, Help, Highscores, RaceHud, Pause) are built in C#:
  - `RacingGameCasaEngine/Screens/*.cs` on `RaceFrontEndScreenBase`;
  - factories in `UI/LegacyMenuUiTheme.cs` and `UI/RaceUiTheme.cs`.

  No screen is described in XAML, and `Content/AssetInfos.json` holds no screen or sprite asset.
- CasaEngine already supports XAML-authored screens catalogued as `.uiscreen` + `.xaml` assets (ADR-0035, ADR-0038):
  - screens are loaded in Strict mode through `XamlUIScreenBase`;
  - values are bound to observable view models with `{dataBinding:MGBinding}`;
  - images are named by `sprite` assets;
  - screens are editable in the CasaEngine editor.
- MGUI XAML has gaps that this migration hits:
  - no event attributes, no source rectangle on `Image`, no indexer in binding paths;
  - border thickness is not a visual-state target;
  - a parent's focus does not restyle its children or siblings.
- A throw-away spike on a copy of the game (2026-10-05) loaded a catalogued `.uiscreen` by name. It resolved `png` → `.texture` → `.sprite` images through `Image.SourceName`. A view model updated every frame drove `TextBlock.Text`, `Image.SourceName` and `RenderTransform.Rotation` (`MGBinding TargetPathOverride=RenderTransform.Rotation`).

## Decision

- **Scope.** Only the 9 screens of RacingGameCasaEngine move to XAML; the legacy RacingGame does not change.
- **Screen assets.** Each screen is a CasaEngine `.uiscreen` + `.xaml` asset catalogued in `Content/AssetInfos.json` and loaded by a screen derived from `XamlUIScreenBase` through `AssetContentManager`.
- **Bindings.** Displayed values come from view models bound with `{dataBinding:MGBinding}`; code does not push values into elements.
- **Race HUD.** The HUD is entirely in XAML:
  - digits are `Image` slots bound to sprite names;
  - the tachometer needle is bound in rotation;
  - texts are bound `TextBlock`s;
  - no custom `OnEndingDraw` drawing remains.
- **Images.** Atlas images are `.sprite` assets (with their `.texture` wrappers) generated by a versioned script in `scripts/`, named in XAML by `Image.SourceName`.
- **Visual states.** Hover, focus and selection keep their exact current look by per-frame restyling in code of elements found by name. MGUI gaps met are reported to drive MGUI's evolution.
- **Editor.** A complete CasaEngine project is created for RacingGameCasaEngine so that the CasaEngine editor opens its content and previews and edits the screens with design-time data.
- **Submodules.** CasaEngine and MGUI are not modified; gaps are reported, never worked around inside the submodules.

## Consequences

- **Reference implementation.** The screens become editable assets and RacingGameCasaEngine follows the engine's screen convention. It is the first shipped consumer of view-model binding in this repository.
- **Code that remains.** Button actions (`AddCommandHandler`), initial focus, per-frame visual-state restyling, view-model refresh from game state, and the 3D car preview rendering stay in code.
- **Fixed slots.** Repeated structures (rows, swatches, digits) become fixed XAML slots bound to flat or nested properties, coupling slot counts to catalogue sizes.
- **Dispose.** Screens must be disposed by the game, since `ScreenStack` never calls `Dispose`, or MGUI's static binding registry keeps them alive.
- **Sprite pipeline.** Sprite assets are regenerated by script; catalogue entries owned by the script carry the `Ui.` prefix and every other entry is left untouched.
- **MGUI gaps.** They are documented in `docs/mgui-gaps-from-rgce-xaml-screens.md`.
