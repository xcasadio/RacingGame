# ADR-0030: RacingGameCasaEngine lights the tunnel with the original's material ambient as an emissive texture

- **Status**: Accepted; amended by ADR-0033 (emissive level 0.6 and shadow factor 1 for the tunnel, replacing the author's 0.5)
- **Date**: 2026-11-17 (the author chose the level of the emissive colour on this date)
- **Source**: this chantier: `ai-agent/tasks/rgce-pbr-handover-tasks.md`, slice S3 (phase 15), decisions T1 to T3 (taken in AUTO mode), open points O1 and O16 (the author's choice of the level), tasks T15.1, T15.2 and T15.4; the original: `RacingGameOriginal/Core/Tracks/Track.cs:84-89`, `Track.cs:1309-1343`, `Content/Shaders/ShadowMap.fx:36` and `:240`; the engine: `CasaEngine/CasaEngine/Content/Shaders/LitPbr.fx:521-557`

## Context

- The original's tunnel material is `roadTunnelMaterial`: ambient 182, diffuse 80, specular 64, with the comment "Use mainly ambient color (tunnel uses lightmaps)". The original lights a pixel as `texture x (A + D x N.L)`; the road and the tunnels cast and receive shadows (`Track.GenerateShadow`, `Track.UseShadow`), and its shadow mask multiplies the image by (0.25, 0.26, 0.27).
- Here the tunnel material is a cooked `lit-pbr` material with a base colour of 80 and no emissive. `lit-pbr` has no ambient colour per material, and the ambient of the race world is the sky cube's irradiance times 0.05. The inside of the tunnel is almost black: 57.1 % of the pixels of the Beginner tunnel audit capture have a luminance under 10 (verification of phase 13; 1.4 % on Advanced and 0.4 % on Expert, 19.7 % on average), as it was before PBR (ADR-0019, Consequences, open point O1 of the plan).
- The engine can carry the term `texture x A`: in `LitPbr.fx` the emissive is `EmissiveColor x EmissiveTexture`, both display colours, decoded together and added to the pixel, and the shadow tint does not touch it.
- The recipes had an emissive colour only, and the cooker wrote no emissive texture.
- None of the 15 reference views of the original is taken in a tunnel; the usual views never enter one.

## Decision

- **T1: the original exports a reference view inside the tunnel.** `--capture-track-audit` of `RacingGameOriginal` gains the opt-in `--tunnel-views`: only one view per track is exported, taken in the longest `Tunnel` range of the road helpers (camera at the road point a quarter of the way into the range, 2 m above it; target the first road point at least 15 m further along, 1.5 m above it; the car is placed at the camera point and not drawn). Without the option the capture is unchanged: the 15 views, their images, `views.json` and `scene.json` are identical to the earlier export, and `artifacts/pbr-handover/s1c/ref` is untouched. The views of the tunnels go to their own folder, `artifacts/pbr-handover/s3/ref-tunnel`; the port replays it with its usual `--capture-reference-views`.
- **T2: the tunnel gets an emissive texture.** `RaceMaterialRecipe` gains `EmissiveTextureFilePath`; `ForRoadTunnel` sets it to the tunnel's diffuse texture and gives `EmissiveColor` the factor below, so that the emissive is the original's `texture x A`. The cooker writes the key `emissive_texture` only for a recipe that names an emissive texture, and its verifier reads an absent key as the null id, so the 98 other materials are cooked as before. No light is added in the tunnel.
- **T3: the level of the emissive colour, `RaceMaterialRecipes.LegacyTunnelEmissiveFactor`, is 0.5 on the three channels (a neutral grey), the author's choice.** It was reached in two steps. First by measurement, between two candidates: (a) 182/255 (0.714), the original's ambient; (b) 182/255 times the shadow colour (0.25, 0.26, 0.27), about 0.18, because the engine's emissive escapes the shadow tint while the original's ambient takes it. Each was cooked in a copy of the game's build folder, outside the repository, and replayed from that copy on the three tunnel views (`nopost`, `linear`). The written rule picked (b) (mean difference per channel with the original 16.39 against 20.56 for (a) and 17.79 before), but (b) barely changed the picture (mean luminance 17.8 for 29.7 in the original) while (a) was clearly too bright (35.0). Then the author asked for intermediate grey values, measured the same way (copies of the build folder, nothing cooked in the repository): mean luminance 23.5, 27.0 and 30.7 for 0.4, 0.5 and 0.6, mean difference per channel 15.88, 16.31 and 17.56, share of pixels under luminance 10 (original 16.7 %) 10.5 %, 7.2 % and 6.1 %. The author chose 0.5. The measurement notes about it that its median over the upper band of the image, walls and ceiling, equals the original's (30.0 against 30.3), that its mean luminance approaches the original's (27.0 for 29.7), and that its difference per channel stays under (b)'s (16.31 against 16.39). Written in the recipe, cooked once in the repository, the three tunnel views replayed from the build folder are byte-identical to those of the measure. The figures are in the sections "After the tunnel emissive" and "After the tunnel emissive level" of `docs/racinggame-3d-capture-bench-local-baseline.md`.

## Consequences

- Amends ADR-0019: its consequence about the tunnel being much darker than the original's, left open as O1, is answered by this record; the tunnel's geometry and the decisions about the helpers are unchanged.
- The cooked `Material.Track.RoadTunnel` has `emissive_color` and `emissive_texture`, and nothing else of the content changes.
- Editing the emissive of `RoadTunnel.material` in the editor changes the game, and running the cooker again reverts it; the level lives in `RaceMaterialRecipes`.
- The emissive is not attenuated by shadows, so the tunnel does not darken further where a shadow falls on it. The original's ambient takes the shadow tint, and a grey of 0.5 is between the two measured candidates, not a derivation from the original's values: neither candidate was right on every part of the image (the original has areas at the level of (b), such as the ceiling of the Beginner tunnel, and areas at the level of (a), such as the left wall of the Expert tunnel).
- The outsides of the tunnels, seen from the road, take the same material and change a little; the bench lists the views of the 15 that change.
- Not settled here: the lens flare of the original, which brightens some tunnel views and which the port does not draw; the colour of the lamps of the tunnel; the chevron signs of the Beginner tunnel (black here, red and white in the original) and the blue tint of the ceiling (open point O16). Decisions T1 and T2 were taken in AUTO mode; the author confirmed the level (T3) and with it this record.
