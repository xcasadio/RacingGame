# ADR-0032: RacingGameCasaEngine balances the light of the race world against the original with an exposure of 0.9

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-11-17
- **Source**: this chantier: `ai-agent/tasks/rgce-render-gaps-tasks.md`, slice R3 (rule of choice written before the measurement, applied mechanically; plan approved by the author on 2026-11-17 in AUTO mode, to be confirmed with this record); the measurement: `artifacts/pbr-handover/s20/r3/` (`table.md`, `step1-rule.txt` to `step4-rule.txt`, one folder per candidate, `sheet-*.png`, `scripts/`); open point O13 of `ai-agent/tasks/rgce-pbr-handover-tasks.md`; ADR-0018, ADR-0031

## Context

Facts verified on 2026-11-17, in the code and in the measurement of this slice:

- The race world renders in `LinearHdr` with the ACES approximation (`TonemapOperator.AcesApprox`) at exposure 1.0, an `AmbientSource.Auto` ambient colour of 0.05 (the author's decision of 2026-11-17, open point O13, phase 13) and `SpecularIntensity` 1 (ADR-0018, ADR-0031). The engine's ACES approximation has no 0.6 factor, so its mid tones rise and its shadows are crushed.
- In `LinearHdr` the engine decodes the ambient colour before it multiplies the irradiance of the sky cube (`CasaEngine/CasaEngine/Framework/Rendering/Environment/ResolvedEnvironmentSettings.cs:106-111`, `EffectiveAmbientColor`): 0.05 displays as about 0.0014 linear. It is 0.05 only in the `legacy` mode. The comment of `RaceWorldFactory.cs` and ADR-0031 say that `lit-pbr` multiplies the irradiance by 5 %: that holds in `legacy`, not in `linear`.
- The reference of the measure before R3 is `artifacts/pbr-handover/s20/ref-engine/linear-a`: mean luminance of the 15 views 104.0 for 96.3 in the original (the figure of 112.3 of O13 predates commit `558a5d9`), mean difference per channel with the original 19.408, probes 16 of 34 (criterion 1), 0 of 4 (criterion 2), 27 of 33 (criterion 3), 3 of 3 (criterion 4).
- The engine offers no per-material control of the sky reflection: the only knob of the world is `SpecularIntensity` (a per-material factor would be an engine change the author did not authorise, A4 of the plan).
- The modes: `legacy` has no tone mapping, so the exposure does not reach it.

## Decision

The linear pipeline of the race world keeps the ACES approximation and takes an exposure of 0.9 (`RaceWorldFactory.LinearExposure`, was 1.0). Nothing else changes: ambient `Auto` 0.05, `SpecularIntensity` 1, light intensity pi, shadows.

The rule of choice, written before the measurement and applied mechanically to four steps (tone curve, ambient, sky reflection, exposure touch-up), is: E is the mean over the 15 views and the 3 channels of the mean absolute difference with the original; (a) a candidate is eligible when criterion 3 stays at 27 of 33 or more and its share of saturated pixels is not above the start state's by more than 0.5 point (steps 3 and 4: and its car box, body only, is not farther from the original than the first candidate's by more than 5 on a channel); (b) when the lowest E of the eligible candidates is not lower than the first candidate's by more than 0.3, the step changes nothing; (c) otherwise, among the eligible candidates within 0.3 of the lowest E, not the first, the one that passes most probes (criteria 1 and 2 added), then the first in the list. Result:

- Step 1, tone curve: ACES at 0.9 (E 19.408 -> 17.947, mean luminance 104.01 -> 98.72 for 96.29 in the original, criterion 1 from 16 to 17 of 34, criterion 3 stays 27 of 33). Reinhard at 1.2, 1.3 and 1.4 (E 19.182, 19.120, 19.353; criterion 1 9, 10, 9 of 34) is measured and not retained: its E is not below ACES at 0.9, and it loses 7 to 8 probes of criterion 1; ACES at 0.6 (E 21.533) is not retained either.
- Step 2, ambient: `Flat` 0.25, 0.28, 0.31 and 0.35 are measured and not retained: the lowest E is 17.823 (`Flat` 0.25) against 17.947, a gain of 0.124, below the 0.3 of the rule. The author's ambient decision (`Auto`, 0.05) is therefore not replaced.
- Step 3, sky reflection: `SpecularIntensity` 0.5 and 0.25 are measured and not retained: E rises to 18.160 and 18.666 and criterion 1 falls to 16 and 13 of 34; the car box gets closer to the original (it loses its sky reflections) but E decides.
- Step 4, exposure touch-up: 0.8 (E 18.004, criterion 1 11 of 34) is not retained; 1.0 is not eligible (car box farther by 7.0 than the first candidate's, above 5). The final state is the state of step 1.

The saturated share is 0 % for every candidate, so the saturation gate never applied.

## Consequences

- The mean luminance of the race in `linear` goes from 104.0 to 98.7 (original 96.3), E from 19.408 to 17.947, and 1 more lit or away probe passes. The comment of `RaceWorldFactory.cs` about the ambient is corrected (decoded in `linear`: about 0.0014). ADR-0018 states the exposure of 1 and is amended by this record; ADR-0031's sentence on 5 % is left as written (a record is not rewritten) and is corrected here.
- Not solved, and left open:
  - The terrain shadow ratio is 0.152 (`Beginner-start`) and 0.139 (`Expert-sector-80`) against 0.284 and 0.267 in the original. Only Reinhard brings it near (0.304 and 0.276 at 1.2), at the cost of E and of criterion 1 (see step 1); criterion 2 stays at 0 of 4 for ACES. Closing it would need a different curve or a change of the shadow colour, not an exposure.
  - The city ratio (`Beginner-sector-20`: 3.171 for 0.276: the shadow probes are brighter than the lit probes, the cobbles lit are darker than in the original, already a known point of O13) and the road ratio (`Beginner-sector-50`: 0.844 for 0.228) are unchanged by the exposure (3.143 and 0.847 at 1.0).
  - The road stays bluish; the faces turned away from the sun are still lit mostly by the sky cube (cold tint).
  - With a `Flat` ambient of 0.25 the chevron signs of `Beginner-sector-50` appear red and white again (seen on the contact sheet, `S2-flat-0.25`), though the rule does not retain it (gain in E 0.124, below 0.3). That is an option left to the author: it costs 1 probe of criterion 1 (16 against 17) and replaces the author's decision of 0.05.
- Reversible: one constant. The tunnel is handled in R4 (its views are measured in the bench document, after this change, for information).
