# ADR-0018: The race world renders in linear HDR with PBR materials

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-11-16
- **Source**: this chantier: `ai-agent/tasks/rgce-pbr-race-world-tasks.md` (D1 to D8, P1 to P9, approved by the author on 2026-11-16), following `docs/racinggame-pbr-preparation.md` and CasaEngine ADR-0116 (detail texture of `lit-pbr`)

## Context

Facts verified on 2026-11-16, in the code at the end of the chantier:

- Before the chantier every material of the race world was a `LitDiffuseMaterial` (Blinn-Phong) tuned by `RacingGameLegacyMaterialTuning`, with a flat white ambient and a per-material reflection cube. The original's own look rests on accidents (shininess 24 everywhere, a global 0.931 darkening, an opaque dark glass, a car body that inherits the road's normal map: `docs/racinggame-pbr-preparation.md` section 4), which the author arbitrated one by one.
- The engine (CasaEngine `48d720a6`) offers `LitPbrMaterial` (metallic-roughness, clear coat, detail texture), a colour pipeline (`Legacy`, `LinearHdr` with tonemapping, FXAA) and image-based lighting generated from a source cube once a `lit-pbr` shader is bound (`CasaEngine/docs/engine/pbr-rendering.md`, `color-pipeline.md`). At equal light intensity the diffuse of a `lit-pbr` surface is pi times darker than a `lit-diffuse` one, and the exposure is global: a world mixing both materials cannot be balanced by the exposure alone.
- The sky pipeline of the race view belongs to the game (`LegacySkyCubeViewPipeline`). The car selection carousel renders its world and its view into a transparent render target and the game's `OpaqueGeometryAlphaViewPipeline` writes the cut-out alpha; the engine's tonemapping always writes an alpha of 1.

## Decision

- **Colour pipeline (D1, D7, D8, P3).** The race world renders in `LinearHdr`: tonemapper `AcesApprox`, exposure 1, bloom and SSAO off, FXAA on. The game's sky pipeline binds the decode of the sky and implements `IScenePhaseViewPipeline`, so the sky is decoded, tonemapped with the scene and anti-aliased; the HUD is not tonemapped. The option `--color-pipeline legacy|linear` (default `linear`) keeps the engine's legacy chain for the capture bench. No global darkening of 7 % (A11): the brightness is set by the exposure.
- **Materials (D2, D3, P1, P2, P9).** Every material of the race world and of the car is a `LitPbrMaterial` built by the one function `RacingGamePbrMaterialMapping.Create`, which holds the family table and the common rules: base colour is the former diffuse colour with alpha forced to 255 (otherwise the material would move to the transparent queue), base and normal textures kept, emissive, queue, alpha cut-off, culling and sampling kept, ambient colour, specular colour and power, reflection cube and reflection blend dropped. Starting values (metallic / roughness): terrain 0 / 1 (detail texture tiled 24 times, from the engine's `lit-pbr` detail texture, P1); road, road underside, tunnel, columns, city ground 0 / 0.85; guard rails 1 / 0.45; lamp posts and signals 1 / 0.5; masonry 0 / 0.9; signs and banners 0 / 0.6; vegetation 0 / 0.95; matte industrial 0 / 0.7; glass of the buildings 1 / 0.05; car paint 0 / 0.5 with a clear coat of 1 and clear-coat roughness 0.05; car chrome 1 / 0.1; car windows 1 / 0.05; car tyres 0 / 0.9; carousel plate 1 / 0.3; default 0 / 0.8. No metallic-roughness texture is authored: scalars per material. `RacingGameLegacyMaterialTuning` is deleted; the import-profile verifier now checks the expected PBR family instead of the reflection hints.
- **Light (P4, P8).** The directional light of the race world and of the carousel has an intensity of pi, in both colour modes, so that a diffuse surface lit head-on keeps the level it had. The environment of both worlds is `AmbientSource.Auto` from the sky cube, ambient and specular intensity 1: the ambient and the reflections are image-based.
- **Accidental behaviours of the original.** A2 (D4): the car body keeps its own normal map `RacerCarNormal.png`, not the one inherited from the cement. A5 (D5): the glass buildings are a mirror (opaque smooth metal reflecting the sky), not dark panels. A10 (D6): palms cast leaf-shaped shadows, from the alpha cut-out of their material. A11 (D7): see above.
- **Carousel (P5).** The car selection carousel receives the PBR materials, the sky environment and the pi light, but stays in the `Legacy` colour pipeline: its view needs a cut-out alpha that the tonemapping does not write. Rendering it in `LinearHdr` is an engine matter (a tonemapping that keeps the alpha and a two-phase carousel pipeline), not done here.
- **What the bench still measures (P7).** The bench replays the original's views in `legacy` and `linear`. Both use the same PBR materials: only the colour chain changes. Compared with the original, geometry (positions, objects, shadow flags: identical to the reference of the start of the chantier), the shadow or light class of the probes and their repeatability remain valid; the colours of both modes are no longer comparable to the original or to the reference captured before the switch.

## Consequences

- The look of the race is now set by a handful of scalars in `RacingGamePbrMaterialMapping`, the key light intensity, the exposure and the sky cube. All of them are starting values, to be tuned by eye by the author. Not fixed, and measured at the end of the chantier: the road is lighter against the terrain than in the original (ratio of the lit road over the lit terrain 0.71 in `linear` against 0.56 before the switch and 0.35 in the original); the terrain shadow ratio in `linear` is lower than in the original (0.14 to 0.17 against 0.27 to 0.28); the faces turned away from the sun are lit by the sky (`building-away-1`). The IBL is derived from the 512 pixel sky cube without mipmaps, whose quality has not been judged on a race.
- Six probes the original shadows (`city-shadow-1` to `-3`, `road-shadow-1` to `-3`) are lit in the port, as they were before the chantier (the probe criterion on the shadow or light class stays at 27 of 33).
- The CasaEngine pointer must stay at or after the commit that has the detail texture of `lit-pbr` (ADR-0116). A material of the race world must go through `RacingGamePbrMaterialMapping.Create`: a `LitDiffuseMaterial` would be pi times brighter than its neighbours at equal light.
- The carousel renders with the `Legacy` chain: the car is lighter there than in the race (the light and the image-based lighting add without compression), and its tint differs slightly from the race.
- The original's glow and lens flare, textures authored per model, and transparent glass remain out of scope.
